## 💡 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions could be considered:

1. Investigate the West region
Since the West generates the highest revenue, analyze what is driving its performance and determine whether successful strategies can be replicated elsewhere.

2. Focus on the 28–37 customer segment
Develop targeted campaigns and product recommendations around the strongest age segment.

3. Leverage high-performing product
Use products such as Vacuum Cleaners, Skipping Ropes, and Running Shoes in promotions and cross-selling strategies.

4. Investigate the North region
The North region generates only about $806K, significantly below the other regions. Further analysis could determine whether this is caused by customer volume, product availability, pricing, or low transaction frequency.

5. Prepare for seasonal peaks
The strong Q1 and Q4 performance suggests that sales planning, inventory, and marketing campaigns should be aligned with periods of higher demand.
